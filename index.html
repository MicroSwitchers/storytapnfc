ONE TAP, THEIR FAVOURITE STORY

Tags & a few essentials

Keep the app open on an unlocked phone while scanning.

1. Link a tag

Letters, numbers, and hyphens. Write this code on the card for manual playback.

2. Make it theirs

Place an NDEF NFC sticker on a toy or card. In child mode, choose Listen for tags, then hold the tag near the phone’s NFC reader.

Android Chrome supports in-app NFC. On other browsers, use story buttons or a code. A URL tag can open the app, then the listener presses Play.

Keep your stories safe

Audio and tag links belong to this browser and site address. Clearing site data can erase them. Export a backup before changing phones, browsers, or domains.

Make yourself comfortable

Uses your device’s voice. Offline voices vary by device.

Use your browser’s Install app or Add to Home Screen option. Open online once before going offline.
